Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SocialHub collects, uses, and protects information when you use our social media management platform. By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, and role within your organization.
  • Authentication data, including login credentials stored using industry-standard security practices.
  • Connected platform data, such as social account identifiers, pages, posts, messages, analytics, and advertising assets authorized through OAuth or API connections.
  • Usage information, including actions taken in the app, feature usage, and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve SocialHub features.
  • Authenticate users and enforce access controls.
  • Publish content and manage engagement on connected platforms at your direction.
  • Monitor performance, troubleshoot issues, and protect against abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the platform, with connected third-party platforms when you authorize integrations, or when required by law. Access is limited to what is necessary for the stated purpose.

4. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary based on data type and organizational settings.

5. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You may disconnect integrated social accounts through the platform where supported. Account changes or deletion requests may be handled by your organization administrator or the SocialHub support contact for your team.

7. Third-party services

Connected social and advertising platforms process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third-party service you connect to SocialHub.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact your organization administrator or the SocialHub support contact provided by your team.
